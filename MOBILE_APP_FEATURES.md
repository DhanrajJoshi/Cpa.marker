## 🚀 How to Use This App

### **Installation on Mobile:**

**Android:**
1. Open app in Chrome
2. Tap menu (⋮) → "Install app"
3. Tap "Install"
4. App appears on home screen

**iOS:**
1. Open in Safari
2. Tap Share button
3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
4. Name the app and tap "Add"

### **Features in Action:**

**Before Going Offline:**
- Browse all sections
- Read about projects & experience
- Review contact form

**When Offline:**
- All previously loaded content visible
- Can type messages in contact form
- Form auto-saves
- Get notified when back online

**Back Online:**
- Pending messages auto-send
- Get success notification
- Everything syncs automatically
